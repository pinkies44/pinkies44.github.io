Privacy Policy

PKB Backup Sync is a private, owner-operated file backup utility.

Data access and purpose

With the account owner's consent, the app uses the Google Drive API's drive.file permission to create and manage files created by, or specifically made available to, the app. File contents and related metadata are accessed only as needed for backup, retrieval, and recovery.

Storage and sharing

Files are stored in the account owner's Google Drive. Authentication credentials are kept in the operator's private configuration, not on this website. The app does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Retention and control

Files remain in the owner's account until removed by the owner or under the owner's backup retention settings. The owner can delete files and revoke the app's access through their Google Account.

Website and contact

This information-only website does not collect personal data, accept file uploads, or use analytics. For questions, use the developer contact provided on the application's OAuth consent screen.
